Beginner tips

Subtitles or dub?

Both are fine. Subtitles keep the Japanese voices, dubs let you multitask. Try episode one both ways.

Where can I watch?

Legal services such as Crunchyroll, Netflix and Prime Video carry many of these. It depends on your country, so search the title on JustWatch.

What if I don't like it?

Give it three episodes. If it still isn't clicking, drop it and try a different mood. That is normal.

Do I watch in order?

For story shows, yes. Each season builds on the last. Movies like Your Name and Spirited Away stand alone.
